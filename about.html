Education

Danni Zhao is an electronic music composer and cross-media sound practitioner currently pursuing a master’s degree in Electronic Music Composition at the Central Conservatory of Music (CCOM), where she studies with Professor Xiaobing Li. In 2021, she was admitted to CCOM as the top-ranked entrant in Electronic Music Composition. She studied with Professors Peng Guan and Qi Qian, alongside additional composition studies with Bo Dai of the Composition Department.

In 2024, she received the National Scholarship and qualified for graduate admission to CCOM through its recommendation-based admission scheme, which exempts eligible students from the entrance examination. She began her master’s studies in 2025.

Earlier distinctions include the Beijing Municipal Government Scholarship and the Shuping Scholarship, which she received for three consecutive years from 2018 to 2020.

Selected platforms & festivals

Her work has been selected for numerous professional platforms in computer music, electroacoustic music, and cross-media art, both internationally and in China. She has been selected for two consecutive editions of the International Computer Music Conference (ICMC), held in Boston, USA (ICMC 2025), and Hamburg, Germany (ICMC 2026). Her work was also selected for the Evening of Performances at the 14th ACM SIGPLAN International Workshop on Functional Art, Music, Modelling and Design (FARM 2026, Indianapolis, USA), an official workshop of the ACM SIGPLAN International Conference on Functional Programming (ICFP 2026).

As part of Processing’s 25th anniversary celebrations, she was selected for the Project Showcase at Processing Community Day 2026 (PCD 2026, Boston/Cambridge, USA), organized by the Future Sketches research group at the MIT Media Lab, Massachusetts Institute of Technology.

Further selections in the United States include the performance program of the 25th Electronic Music Midwest (EMM 2027, Kansas City, USA); the Performance program of MOXsonic 2027 — Missouri Experimental Sonic Arts Festival, hosted by the University of Central Missouri; and the performance program of TURN UP Multimedia Festival 2027 (Tucson, Arizona, USA), hosted by the University of Arizona School of Music.

Her work was also selected for the WAVE Electroacoustic Music Festival 2026 at the University of Oregon, USA, and the official Live Performance program of NOIS//E 2026 Festival & Symposium in Milan, Italy.

Another international selection was the “KARMA” exhibition of the International Electroacoustic Music Exhibition MUSLAB 2026, presented at the Museo de Manta in Manta, Ecuador, South America.

In China, her work has been selected for the Electronic Music Marathon at the inaugural CCF Conference on Computational Art (CCAC 2025), the Central Conservatory of Music program of the Electronic Music Marathon at the 3rd World Summit on Music Intelligence (SOMI 2026), and the sound installation exhibition of the international conference “Thinking with Sound: Constructing a Noisy and Sonorous Encyclopedia” at the Southern University of Science and Technology.

Awards

In professional competitions, Zhao has received the Grand Prize (Highest Score) at the 34th International Competition for Young Musicians “Città di Barletta” in Italy; First Prize at the 15th Chinese Collegiate Computing Competition; Second Prize at the IEMC 2022 International Electronic Music Competition; an Outstanding Award at the Electronic Music Composition Competition of the 2nd World Summit on Music Intelligence (SOMI 2023); Third Prize at the 2025 Hangzhou International Electronic Music Festival · Hangzhou International Electronic Music Composition Competition; and First Prize at the 10th Sibelius International Composition Competition. Additional honors include a Gold Prize at the Debussy International Music Competition in the United Kingdom, a Gold Prize at the Erik Satie International Music Competition, and a Platinum Prize at the Max Bruch International Competition.

Film, stage & cross-media practice

Alongside her independent artistic practice, Zhao has contributed to documentary film, theater, and cross-media projects. Film-scoring projects to which she contributed have received awards, nominations, or selections through a range of film festivals and professional programs, including the FIRST International Film Festival; the CFDG Young Directors Support Program; the “Annual Shengfang Honor” in the Animated Short Film category at the 2nd Shengfang Plan Honors Ceremony of the China Film Foundation’s Women’s Film Special Fund; the “Little Universe Award” of Bilibili’s Light-Chasing Project; the 24th Beijing Film Academy Animation Awards; the 31st College Student Film Festival of the Beijing International Film Festival; and the ReelFocus Real Image Initiative.

She worked on music mixing for CCTV’s large-scale documentary series Reaching the Summit of the Earth Again and Zhejiang Television’s first 8K documentary Feilai Peak. The latter was nominated for the “2024 Top Ten Most Influential Chinese Documentaries” distinction by China’s National Radio and Television Administration.

She served as an arranger for Gan Da Ying, a production by the Tianjin People’s Art Theatre, and composed music for Yun Tianming’s Fairy Tales, a science-fiction-themed concert based on The Three-Body Problem.

As part of a collaborative team, she also contributed to arrangements for a large-scale percussion ensemble of more than 100 performers at the Jianli Marimba Art Festival’s “Cliff Concert.”

A collaborative cross-media work was exhibited at the Royal College of Art in the United Kingdom, while an installation work was presented at ZhaoDai 2026 in Aranya, Qinhuangdao, Hebei, China, alongside more than 30 groups of artists from China and abroad representing diverse electronic music and club culture scenes.

Current practice

Her current practice focuses on electronic music composition, computer music, sound installation, and cross-media art, incorporating real-time sound analysis, interactive systems, spatial audio, audiovisual relationships, and physical media.

教育经历

赵雪丹妮，电子音乐作曲者、跨媒介声音实践者，现为中央音乐学院电子音乐作曲专业硕士研究生，师从李小兵教授。

2021年以专业第一名考入中央音乐学院电子音乐作曲专业，先后师从关鹏教授、钱琦教授，并跟随作曲系代博老师学习作曲；

2024年获国家奖学金及中央音乐学院推荐免试攻读研究生资格，2025年进入硕士研究生阶段学习。

早年曾获北京市政府奖学金，并于2018至2020年连续三年获叔蘋奖学金。

影视、舞台与跨媒介实践

除个人创作外，亦参与影视、舞台及跨媒介艺术实践。参与创作的影视配乐作品曾获奖或入围FIRST青年电影展、CFDG中国青年导演扶持计划、中国电影基金会女性电影专项基金第二届盛放计划荣誉盛典动画短片单元“年度盛放荣誉”、哔哩哔哩寻光计划“小宇宙奖”、第24届北京电影学院动画学院奖、北京国际电影节·第31届大学生电影节及ReelFocus真实影像计划等。

曾参与CCTV大型系列纪录片《再登地球之巅》及浙江卫视首部8K纪录片《飞来峰》音乐混音，其中《飞来峰》获广电总局“2024中国影响力十大纪录片”提名；

担任天津人民艺术剧院《赶大营》编曲，并为《三体》科幻主题音乐会“云天明的童话”作曲。

作为团队成员，参与箭丽马林巴艺术节“崖壁音乐会”百余人大型打击乐团合奏曲目改编。

跨媒介合作作品曾于英国皇家艺术学院（Royal College of Art）展出；装置作品于河北秦皇岛·阿那亚「招待会2026」展出，与30余组来自海内外、横跨不同电子音乐与俱乐部文化场景的艺人共同呈现。

创作方向

目前创作主要涉及电子音乐、计算机音乐、声音装置及跨媒介艺术，实践涵盖实时声音分析、交互系统、空间声音、视听关系与物理媒介等方向。
