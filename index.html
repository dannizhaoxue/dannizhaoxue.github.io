Danni Zhao 赵雪丹妮

Danni Zhao is an electronic music composer and cross-media sound practitioner currently pursuing a master’s degree in Electronic Music Composition at the Central Conservatory of Music (CCOM), where she studies with Professor Xiaobing Li.

Her current practice focuses on electronic music composition, computer music, sound installation, and cross-media art, incorporating real-time sound analysis, interactive systems, spatial audio, audiovisual relationships, and physical media.

Weathering

June 2026

Weathering is a six-minute networked digital score for twenty performers across Beijing Xicheng, Beijing Haidian and Wuhan. Earth, water, fire and wind become materials for a shared progression, realised through listening and individual judgment.
